Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click on the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. You will receive an email with instructions to reset your password.
Q: How can I view my attendance records?
A: After logging in, navigate to the "Attendance" section in the sidebar. You can view your overall attendance percentage and subject-wise details there.
Q: Where can I find study materials?
A: Study materials including notes, PPTs, textbooks, and previous year questions are available in the "Study Material" section of your dashboard.
Q: How do I submit assignments?
A: Go to the "Assignments" section, select your subject, and click on the assignment you want to submit. Follow the instructions to upload your work.
Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement?
A: The minimum attendance requirement is 75% for each subject. Students falling below this may not be eligible to appear for examinations.
